# Awesome Federal Contracting [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> A curated list of free official sources, tools, guides, and help for small businesses that sell to the U.S. federal government.

Federal contracting info is spread across dozens of .gov sites and vendor tools. This list collects the ones that are actually useful to a small contractor, with the official sources first.

## Official Sources

- [SAM.gov](https://sam.gov/) - Official home of federal contract opportunities, entity registration, and Unique Entity IDs (UEI).
- [SAM.gov Contract Opportunities search](https://sam.gov/search/?index=opp) - Search active solicitations, sources sought notices, and awards.
- [SAM.gov Opportunities API](https://open.gsa.gov/api/get-opportunities-public-api/) - Free public API for pulling contract opportunity notices.
- [USAspending.gov](https://www.usaspending.gov/) - Searchable federal spending data, including contract awards by agency, NAICS, and recipient.
- [FPDS](https://www.fpds.gov/) - Federal Procurement Data System, the source of record for contract award data.
- [Acquisition Gateway](https://acquisitiongateway.gov/) - GSA's hub for acquisition resources and agency forecasts.
- [GSA eLibrary](https://www.gsaelibrary.gsa.gov/) - Look up GSA Multiple Award Schedule contracts, contractors, and categories.
- [GSA Forecast of Contracting Opportunities](https://acquisitiongateway.gov/forecast) - Planned procurements published by participating agencies.
- [SBIR.gov](https://www.sbir.gov/) - Solicitations, awards, and guides for SBIR/STTR small business research funding.
- [Grants.gov](https://www.grants.gov/) - Federal grant opportunities (not contracts, but often confused with them).

## Registration and Certification

- [SAM.gov entity registration help](https://sam.gov/content/entity-registration) - Official steps for registering an entity and getting a UEI.
- [Federal Service Desk](https://www.fsd.gov/) - Official help desk for SAM.gov problems.
- [NAICS code search (Census)](https://www.census.gov/naics/) - Find the NAICS codes that describe your work.
- [SBA size standards](https://www.sba.gov/federal-contracting/contracting-guide/size-standards) - Check whether you qualify as small for a NAICS code.
- [MySBA Certifications](https://certifications.sba.gov/) - Apply for 8(a), HUBZone, WOSB/EDWOSB, and VOSB/SDVOSB certifications.
- [SBA Dynamic Small Business Search](https://dsbs.sba.gov/) - Government buyers search this profile database; keep yours complete.

## Small Business Programs and Set-Asides

- [SBA federal contracting overview](https://www.sba.gov/federal-contracting) - SBA's entry point for selling to the government.
- [8(a) Business Development](https://www.sba.gov/federal-contracting/contracting-assistance-programs/8a-business-development-program) - Program for socially and economically disadvantaged small businesses.
- [HUBZone program](https://www.sba.gov/federal-contracting/contracting-assistance-programs/hubzone-program) - Set-asides for firms in historically underutilized business zones.
- [Women-Owned Small Business program](https://www.sba.gov/federal-contracting/contracting-assistance-programs/women-owned-small-business-federal-contract-program) - WOSB and EDWOSB set-asides.
- [Veteran contracting assistance](https://www.sba.gov/federal-contracting/contracting-assistance-programs/veteran-contracting-assistance-programs) - VOSB and SDVOSB programs.
- [Set-aside types explained (OppBeacon guide)](https://oppbeacon.com/guides/set-aside-types-8a-hubzone-sdvosb-wosb) - Plain-English comparison of 8(a), HUBZone, SDVOSB, and WOSB set-asides.
- [Open opportunities by set-aside (OppBeacon)](https://oppbeacon.com/set-asides) - Free pages listing current SAM.gov notices grouped by set-aside type.

## Opportunity Search and Alerts

Free:

- [SAM.gov saved searches](https://sam.gov/search/?index=opp) - Save a search while signed in and get email notifications.
- [Open opportunities by state (OppBeacon)](https://oppbeacon.com/states) - Free pages of current SAM.gov notices by place of performance.
- [Due this week (OppBeacon)](https://oppbeacon.com/due-this-week) - Free page of open notices with response deadlines in the next seven days.

Commercial (most offer a free tier or trial; check each site for current pricing):

- [GovTribe](https://govtribe.com/) - Opportunity, award, and vendor intelligence with pipeline tracking.
- [HigherGov](https://www.highergov.com/) - Federal, state, and local opportunities, awards, and grants with search alerts.
- [GovWin IQ (Deltek)](https://www.deltek.com/en/government-contracting/govwin) - Enterprise market intelligence, including pre-RFP forecasts.
- [BGOV (Bloomberg Government)](https://about.bgov.com/) - Contracting, policy, and budget intelligence for larger teams.
- [GovSpend](https://govspend.com/) - Spending and purchase-order data, strongest for state and local.
- [OppBeacon](https://oppbeacon.com/) - SAM.gov alerts filtered to your NAICS and set-asides, with an AI bid/no-bid summary that cites the solicitation text.

Comparisons:

- [SAM.gov alerts vs. third-party tools (OppBeacon)](https://oppbeacon.com/compare/sam-gov-alerts) - What SAM.gov's built-in notifications do and do not cover.
- [GovTribe alternatives (OppBeacon)](https://oppbeacon.com/compare/govtribe-alternative) - Vendor-written comparison; read with that in mind.
- [HigherGov alternatives (OppBeacon)](https://oppbeacon.com/compare/highergov-alternative) - Vendor-written comparison; read with that in mind.

## Pricing and Past Performance Research

- [USAspending award search](https://www.usaspending.gov/search) - See who won similar work, for how much, and when.
- [GSA CALC+](https://buy.gsa.gov/pricing/) - Labor category rates from GSA schedule contracts, useful for pricing services.
- [CPARS](https://www.cpars.gov/) - Contractor performance assessments (contractors can view their own).
- [SBA Small Business Procurement Scorecard](https://www.sba.gov/document/support-small-business-procurement-scorecard-overview) - Agency small business goaling results.

## Regulations

- [Acquisition.gov](https://www.acquisition.gov/) - The FAR and agency supplements, searchable.
- [FAR Part 19 - Small Business Programs](https://www.acquisition.gov/far/part-19) - The rules behind set-asides.
- [DFARS](https://www.acquisition.gov/dfars) - Defense supplement to the FAR.
- [Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/) - Proposed and final rule changes affecting contracting.

## Free Help

- [APEX Accelerators](https://www.apexaccelerators.us/) - Free one-on-one counseling for government contracting (formerly PTACs), funded by DoD. Use the office locator on the site.
- [Small Business Development Centers](https://www.sba.gov/local-assistance/resource-partners/small-business-development-centers-sbdc) - Free business advising, many with contracting specialists.
- [SBA local assistance finder](https://www.sba.gov/local-assistance) - SBA district offices, SBDCs, SCORE, and Women's Business Centers.

## Learning Resources

- [SBA Contracting Guide](https://www.sba.gov/federal-contracting/contracting-guide) - Step-by-step basics from SBA.
- [SBA Learning Platform](https://www.sba.gov/sba-learning-platform) - Free self-paced courses, including government contracting.
- [Federal Acquisition Institute](https://www.fai.gov/) - Training used by the federal acquisition workforce.
- [How to find opportunities on SAM.gov (OppBeacon guide)](https://oppbeacon.com/guides/how-to-find-federal-contract-opportunities-on-sam-gov) - Walkthrough of SAM.gov search filters.
- [Sources sought vs. RFI vs. RFP vs. RFQ (OppBeacon guide)](https://oppbeacon.com/guides/sources-sought-vs-rfi-vs-rfp-vs-rfq) - What each notice type means and how to respond.
